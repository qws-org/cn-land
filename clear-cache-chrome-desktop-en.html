Chrome  

  1. Click on the browser settings burger icon in the top right corner of the screen

  2. In the menu that opens, select the option Settings.
  3. In the left-hand menu, select Privacy and Security.
  4. In the opened tab, click on the section Delete browsing data. 
  5. In the new window that appears, check the checkbox Cookie Time Range and click the button Delete data.
