Yandex Browser (Desktop)

  1. Open Yandex Browser.

  2. Click the menu button (☰) in the top-right corner.

  3. Hover over History. 

  4. Click Clear history from the submenu. 

  5. In the Clear browsing data popup:

    • Set Time range to The beginning of time.

    • Check only the following:

      • Cookies and other site data and plugins

      • Files stored in cache

     

  6. Click Clear.
