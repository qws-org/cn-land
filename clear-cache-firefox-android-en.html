Firefox browser (Android)

Quick Cache Clearing

  1. Open the Firefox browser on your Android device. Tap the three dots in the top right corner.

  2. Select Settings from the dropdown menu.

  3. Scroll down and tap Delete browsing data under the Privacy and security section.

  4. Check Cached images and files (and any other data you wish to remove). Then tap Delete browsing data to confirm.
