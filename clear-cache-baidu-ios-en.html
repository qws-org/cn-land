Baidu App (iOS)

  1. Open the Baidu app → Tap the "My" (我的) tab at the bottom right.

  2. Tap the “Clear Cache” (清理缓存) option in the menu.

  3. On the cache screen, you’ll see how much space is used. Tap "Safe Clean" (放心清理) to remove all cached data.

    Optional: You can also clean specific categories:

    • Baidu cache only (百度专清) 

    • Images (图片专清)

    • Videos (视频专清)

    • Large files (大文件专清) 

  4. Confirm the cleaning when prompted.
    The app will delete cached data immediately
