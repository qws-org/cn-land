Yandex Browser (iOS)

  1. Open the Yandex Browser app. 

  2. Tap the three lines (≡) icon at the bottom of the screen.

  3. Select Settings.

  4. In the Privacy section, find and tap Clear Data.

  5. In the list that appears, check:

    • Cookies

    • Cache

  6. Tap Clear.

  7. A confirmation popup will appear saying:
    "Are you sure you want to clear data?"
    — tap Yes to confirm.

  8. Close the app and open it again for the changes to take effect.

How to close the app: swipe up from the bottom of the screen (or double-press the Home button), find Yandex Browser in the app switcher, and swipe it up to close.
