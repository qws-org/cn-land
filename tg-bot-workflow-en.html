Telegram Setup Instructions

  1. Open the Telegram app.

    Search for @YPGameBot in the search bar. Then tap on the Open button.

  2. In the bot chat, tap on the Start button to initiate the interaction.

  3. Tap on the Play button to continue.

  4. In the pop-up window, tap on the Launch button.

  5. You will be redirected to the YPGameBot in the Telegram app.

  6. How to exit the bot: To close the app, simply tap the cross in the upper-right corner of the screen or double-tap the area outside the app or swipe up from the bottom of the screen (or double-press the Home button).

If the app does not open?

  1. Tap the three dots in the upper-right corner of the screen. In the window that appears, select Reload Page.

  2. Check your internet connection.

  3. Try closing and reopening Telegram. Sometimes simply restarting the app helps fix minor issues.

  4. Update your Telegram app to the latest version.
