Opera (iOS)

  1. Open the Opera app.

  2. Tap the Profile icon or (⋯) Menu at the bottom.

  3. Tap ⚙️ Settings.

  4. Scroll down and tap Clear Browser Data.

  5. Select:

    • Cookies and Site Data

    • Cached Images and Files

  6. Tap Clear.
    A confirmation popup will appear — tap Clear Selected Data to confirm.

  7. Restart Opera and open the site again.

To restart Opera: swipe up from the bottom (or double-press the Home button), find Opera in the app switcher, swipe it away to close it, then open it again from the home screen.
