PWA Installation (Android)

  1. Open the website in your browser.
  2. Tap the menu icon (three vertical dots ⋮) in the top-right corner of the browser.
  3. In the menu, tap “Add to Home screen”.
  4. A prompt will appear with two options: “Install” or “Create shortcut”.
    Choose “Install” to install it like a native app
  5. A popup will appear — tap “Install” or “Add” to confirm.
  6. The app will now appear on your home screen.
