How to Reinstall a PWA on iOS

  1. Go to your home screen and locate the PWA app icon.
  2. Tap and hold the icon until a menu appears or the icons start to jiggle.
  3. Tap “Remove App” or “Delete Bookmark”.
  4. Confirm by tapping “Delete”.
  5. The PWA will be removed from your device.
  6. Open the website in your browser.
  7. Tap the Share icon (a square with an arrow pointing up ⤴️).
  8. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
  9. Optionally, change the app name if you want.
  10. Tap "Add" in the top-right corner.
  11. The app will now appear on your home screen like a native app.
