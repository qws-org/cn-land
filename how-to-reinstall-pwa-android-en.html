🔄 Reinstalling PWA (Android)

📌 Step 1: Uninstall the Existing PWA

  1. Go to your home screen and locate the app icon.
  2. Tap and hold the app icon until a menu appears.
  3. From the menu, tap “Uninstall” or “Remove App”.
  4. Confirm the action if prompted — the app will be removed from your device.

📲 Step 2: Install the PWA Again

  1. Open the website in your browser.
  2. Tap the menu icon (three vertical dots ⋮) in the top-right corner.
  3. In the menu, tap “Add to Home screen”.
  4. A popup will appear with options like “Install” or “Create shortcut” — choose “Install”.
  5. Tap “Install” or “Add” to confirm.
  6. The app will now appear on your home screen.
