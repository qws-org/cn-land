Chrome (android)

Quick Cache Clearing

  1. Open Chrome on your phone.
  2. Tap the three dots (⋮) in the top right corner.
  3. Go to Settings.
  4. Scroll down and select Privacy and security.
  5. Tap Delete browsing data.
  6. Under the Basic tab: Check Cached images and files, you can also select Cookies and Browsing history if needed.
  7. At the top, choose the time range — for example, All time.
  8. Tap Clear data.
